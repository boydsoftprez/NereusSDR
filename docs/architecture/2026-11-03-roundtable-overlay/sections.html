NereusSDR · desktop, iPhone, iPad and web

Roundtable overlay

Interactive design proposal. Stations and readings are examples. No radio is connected and nothing is published. Desktop uses a real capture of the app with a live pan drawn over it; the phones and iPad use the iPhone board's own engine; the web page uses the real page's own stylesheet and markup with scripted stations.

Desktop

Click the card for its menu. Click another frequency's tag to see who is there and how they read, without moving your slice; tune from there. Clicking the band itself still tunes. The card sits past the flag, away from the stations around your dial; where the band has no room there, it comes back beside the dial.

iPhone

Tap the card for its choices. Tap another tag to see that frequency without moving your slice. Drag the band as usual. The narrow strip beside the flag now carries readings, which it could not before.

Beside the flag

The usual case, at the real pan height: only a narrow strip is free.

Taller pan

Pan dragged taller: the card fits under the flag at its full width.

Reading the sheet

Tap Read reports, or the flag's Roundtable line.

Landscape

A short, wide pan: fewer rows, with the rest one tap away.

iPad

The pan works as on the iPhone, with the desktop's roomier card under the flag. The column is already beside the band, so tapping another frequency's tag opens it there, in the Roundtable applet, with no pop-up. Hide the column with the top right button and the same view opens over the pan. Widen adds the list of every frequency.

Applet in the column

Under the S-Meter, as today. Tap the Double tag on the band to view it without tuning, then Back to my slice.

Widened

Every frequency on the left, with doubles marked and named. Tap one to view it; your own stays highlighted.

Web page

The public page already browses without touching a radio, so it needs no Tune button. The changes: a double is marked in the list and on the band map, each reading gets the same meter under its row with S and dBm on one line, and at phone width the room says who is on frequency, because the list is not beside it there. Everything here works, including bands, search, sort, quiet stations, distance, bearing and signal.
