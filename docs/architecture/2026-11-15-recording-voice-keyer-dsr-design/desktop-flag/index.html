Record and Play on the desktop slice flag

Built on a real capture of the desktop. Record and Play are round buttons in the flag's side column, the same as the lock, in the order you picked on the phone: lock, Record, Play, then ··· at the bottom. On slices B to D the close button sits on top, as it does today.

Things to try
  • Click Record (the dot). It blinks red and the time counts beside it. Click Transmit (MOX) while it records: your own voice goes in too. Click Record again to stop; the recording is kept on the Core.
  • Click Play (the triangle): your latest recording plays on this computer's speaker. Transmit while it plays and it goes out on the air instead of your mic. The button becomes a blue stop square, with the time beside it. Click it to stop.
  • Right-click Play: your last eight recordings, then All recordings…, Show in Finder and Recording settings….
  • Set Core to "On the Pi in the shack": Show in Finder stays in the menu but is dimmed, since the files are on the Pi. "Not connected" dims Record and Play.
  • Rest the pointer on a button for its tip.
