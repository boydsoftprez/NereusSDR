Recording from the desktop and the iPhone together

Both are on the same Core, looking at the same slice A. The recording is made on the Core, so there is only ever one, and both flags show it. Either one can start it and either one can stop it. Playing back is not shared: each plays on its own speaker, and transmitting while one plays sends that recording on the air instead of your mic.

Things to try
  • Tap Record on the iPhone's flag. The desktop flag starts blinking at the same moment, with the same time. Stop it from the desktop: both stop, and both say where it went.
  • After it stops, each shows "Kept" with Play. Play on the iPhone plays it on the phone only; the desktop stays quiet unless you play it there too.
  • Turn on "Start by itself when I transmit". Both Record buttons show a hollow red ring: armed, waiting. Click Transmit: recording starts on both by itself. Then click "Two quiet minutes pass": it stops by itself and goes back to waiting.
  • Tapping Record while it is waiting starts a recording at once, as usual.
  • Start a recording playing on one of them, then transmit from that same one: it goes out on the air instead of your mic, and its playing label turns red with ON AIR. Stop transmitting and it carries on in your speaker. On the iPhone, Others picks which recording, so any of them can go out, not only the latest.
