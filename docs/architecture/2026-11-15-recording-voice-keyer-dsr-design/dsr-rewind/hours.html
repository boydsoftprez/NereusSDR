Digital Spectrum Recorder: getting around hours

The Digital Spectrum Recorder on the Core keeps 3½ hours here (10 GB at this pan width). Pull the time strip up a little to go back, as you do today, and a map of every kept hour appears, shaded by how busy the band was. Click or drag on it to jump anywhere. Click any clock time on the strip to type a time. Past the 20 minutes this window holds, the Core redraws the waterfall first, which takes a moment. Click a signal to play it; red LIVE comes back to the present. On a phone, turn it sideways; the picture scrolls left and right.
