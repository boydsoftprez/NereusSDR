The small Recordings panel

Built on a real capture of the desktop. Added from Containers, Applets, it sits in the right-hand column like any other panel; here it is under RX, with TX moved down below it. It is for quick listening. Managing many recordings is the Recordings window's job.

Things to try
  • Click a row's play button: it plays on this computer's speaker (transmit while it plays to send it on the air), with a blue bar showing how far along it is. Click it again to stop. Double-clicking a row plays it too.
  • A "Whole pan" stretch opens on the waterfall instead, the way rewind plays the past. "Sound" and "Contact" recordings just play.
  • Right-click a row: Play, Rename, Save a copy here, Show in Finder, Delete.
  • Click "Record on the flag" above: the recording in progress shows at the top in red, with a Stop button. Stop it and it joins the list.
  • "All recordings…" opens the Recordings window for search, sorting and deleting many.
  • Set Core to "Not connected": the panel stays, dimmed, with a note.
