VOICE beside TUNE and MOX on the desktop

Built on a real capture of the desktop. The TX panel's TUNE and MOX row gains a third button, VOICE, that opens your eight messages, like the VOICE button beside PTT on the phone.

Things to try
  • Click VOICE: your eight messages open just above it. Click one to send it on the air. The box closes, VOICE turns red, shows the message, and fills from left to right as it plays.
  • Click the red VOICE, press Escape, or click MOX to stop. At the end it goes back to receive by itself.
  • Turn on Repeat in the box: between sends VOICE counts down the seconds to the next one.
  • An empty message, or "Voice keyer panel", opens the full voice keyer panel where you record, import and rename.
  • Set Transmit to "With the iPhone": VOICE dims and the box offers Take transmit.
