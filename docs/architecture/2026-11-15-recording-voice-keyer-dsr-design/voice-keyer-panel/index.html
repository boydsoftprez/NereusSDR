Voice keyer: three shapes

Interactive. All three panels share the same eight messages, so a message recorded in one shows in the others. Sending a message transmits it, and the radio goes back to receive when it ends. Stop, Escape or PTT stops it. Keys: Ctrl+Shift+F1 to F8 send, Ctrl+Shift+F10 stops. Left: today's unwired panel and the CW memory panel above it, captured from the app.
