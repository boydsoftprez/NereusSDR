Record, Play and the voice keyer on the iPad

Built on a real iPad capture, with a pan drawn in (the capture was taken with no radio connected). The iPad takes the phone's design: Record and Play in the flag's side column, the playing bar under the flag, VOICE beside PTT. The column on the right can hold a Recordings panel, like the desktop's.

Things to try
  • Tap the dot beside the flag to record (it blinks red, with the time under it); tap again to keep it. It shows at the top of the Recordings panel while it records.
  • Tap the triangle to play the latest on the iPad's speaker; the blue bar under the flag shows it, with Others for the last eight. Or tap any row's play button in the Recordings panel.
  • While something plays, tap PTT: it goes out on the air instead of your mic, and the bar turns red with ON AIR. Tap PTT again and it carries on in your speaker.
  • Tap VOICE beside PTT for your eight messages. Tapping one sends it; tap VOICE or PTT to stop.
  • "All recordings" in the panel goes to Tools, Recordings: the phone's page, wider. Recording settings is the phone's page too, in Setup.
