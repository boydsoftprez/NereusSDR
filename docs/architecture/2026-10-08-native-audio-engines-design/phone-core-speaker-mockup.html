iPhone: Sound panel with the Core speaker

The panel the band's speaker button opens, as built on this branch (Mute this phone, Radio speaker, Play the band through), with a new Core speaker section under Radio speaker: its level and Mute Core speaker, nothing else. Choosing the Core's device stays in the desktop's Setup. Moving the slider here moves it on every desktop window too. Drag the sliders and tap the switches. The two icons are stand-ins for the phone's own SF Symbols ("radio", and "hifispeaker" for the Core speaker); the Core speaker's cyan matches its slider on the desktop card.

What each shows. Playing: level and mute work as on the desktop card. Unplugged: the section stays usable and says where it plays meanwhile, as the desktop card does. No sound card: option 1 leaves the section out until a card is plugged into the Core, then it appears by itself; option 2 keeps it greyed with its reason. The desktop Setup card shows either way, since Setup is where you go to set one up.
