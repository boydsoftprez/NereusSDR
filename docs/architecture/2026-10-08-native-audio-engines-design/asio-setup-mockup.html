Mockup controls (not part of the app):
Things to try. Computer: switch to Mac or Linux to see their Driver lists and the interface listed by pairs; on Linux with PulseAudio, PipeWire shows greyed out as not running. Outputs: pick a Voicemeeter pair for the speakers while the headphones and mic sit on the Focusrite (one ASIO driver at a time, so it asks first). Microphone: pick AirPods, then disconnect them. Digital modes: give VAX 2 an ASIO pair, install VB-CABLE B and watch it appear, uninstall VB-CABLE A while VAX 3 uses it. ASIO control panel (Outputs, Device details): change the buffer size in the driver's window and press OK; tick "one buffer size only" to see a driver that allows just one. Delay and buffer numbers are examples, not measurements.
