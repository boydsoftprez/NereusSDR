Mockup controls (not part of the app):
Things to try. Pick the USB speaker on the Core speaker card, then untick "USB speaker plugged into the Core": the list and the card change by themselves, and ticking it again brings it back. Untick the jack and the USB speaker to see a Core with no sound card. Switch "This window" to see the card greyed while the Core is unreachable, and gone from a window that runs the radio itself. Device names are examples of what a Raspberry Pi reports; delay numbers are examples, not measurements.

The radio's built-in amplifier for its speaker jacks. Muting the radio speaker also switches it off. Off while transmitting keeps it on for CW and Tune, so you still hear the sidetone. Switching it can make a pop.
