NereusSDR on iPhone

Lock screen and Dynamic Island, close up

The Live Activity card as the app draws it today, next to the desktop's workspace colors. Apple draws everything around the card (the clock, the island's black, the card's outline and shape), so this is the one place our colors sit beside Apple's. The frequency stays cyan and the slice colors stay as they are. Buttons work: mute, Cancel, Reconnect. StandBy shows this same card at twice the size; the black wallpaper stands in for it.

What changes on the card

  • Card: the desktop panel's near-black in place of the navy, with the panel's grey edge. It keeps iOS's own see-through, so a bright wallpaper still tints it slightly, as it does today.
  • Keyed card: the desktop's TX badge red under the whole card, with the muted red edge. The color table alone would have turned it grey, so it is set by hand.
  • UNKEY: the desktop's stronger TX red, the one red the desktop allows only for MOX and TX (JJ, 2026-10-21). Today's bright red is softened to it.
  • Speaker: the desktop's raised button; muted, it lights in the desktop's amber, as today but toned down.
  • Reconnect: the desktop's blue glass. Cancel uses the muted red set.
  • Link chip and badges: rounded like the desktop's chips; the live dot is the desktop's green instead of the bright mint.
  • Signal bar: a muted blue up to S9 and the desktop's green past it, from the same color table, in place of the bright cyan and green.
  • Island: its black is Apple's and stays. Only what sits in it changes: the TX badge and timer, the chip and the buttons.
