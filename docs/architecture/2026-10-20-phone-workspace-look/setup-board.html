Switches when on

What moves, and what stays

  • Panels: the desktop's panel gradient and border, rounded 10, in place of the iPhone's grey cards.
  • Section names in the desktop's group-title grey, like the titles on the desktop's Setup group boxes.
  • A picked choice gets the desktop's blue glass selection, the same as a picked mode on the flag. The check mark stays, so the choice still reads without color.
  • Switches when on: blue, the same as a ticked box in the desktop's Setup (JJ, 2026-10-20). Green stays for a DSP function that is running. A switch that can't be used right now stays in place, greyed, with the reason under it.
  • iPad: the Setup list sits on the left and the open page beside it, as in the desktop's Setup window (JJ, 2026-10-20). An iPhone, or an iPad sharing its screen narrowly, keeps the single list with pages that slide in.
  • Numbers sit in the desktop's dark inset field. Tapping one still opens the number pad at the foot of the tab, as today.
  • Kept as they are: the iPhone's back button and big page title, swipe back from the left edge, the Core / This phone / Both marks (restyled as chips), and every word on every page.
