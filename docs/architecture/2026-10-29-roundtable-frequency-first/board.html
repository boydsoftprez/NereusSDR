Behavior represented on this board

People, not rooms

Each callsign marks that operator’s frequency. The active slice sets your receiving neighborhood. A tag tunes on the desktop and phone; the public map browses without tuning. Nearby lists show the offset and the age of the last presence update.

±5 kHz, evaluated per message

The boundary is inclusive. A sender 5,000 Hz away reaches you; one 5,001 Hz away does not. Filtering is pairwise: two overlapping neighborhoods do not relay a third person’s messages. The example-message button makes this visible.

History and public presence

Tuning inserts a divider and retains earlier received messages with sender frequency and time. Public visitors cannot post. Hiding the station removes its presence and disables posting. Loss of connection preserves history, dims presence and disables Send.

Demo scope

First run, turns, messages, phone keyboard, popout, private archive and helper review are interactive local examples below. Station sign-in, PTT, readings and destructive actions are simulated. No radio, network service, browser authentication or durable settings are exercised.
