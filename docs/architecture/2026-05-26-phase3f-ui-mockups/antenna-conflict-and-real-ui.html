Your proposal refined with TX safety, then proper UI mockups for the affected surfaces.

§1 The conflict-resolution refinement

Your idea: auto-switch all subscribed slices on the conflicting chain to the new slice's antenna. Yes, but with one safety carve-out: never silently re-route a TX-bound slice's antenna. TX-bound antenna selection is RF-critical (wrong antenna could trip SWR, hit the wrong feedline, in worst case damage the PA). Everything else can flip silently with an undo toast.

The policy:
  ● Adding a slice with a new antenna preference, and there's an idle chain → use the idle chain, no conflict.
  ● Both chains active, NEITHER on the wanted antenna, conflict chain has only RX slices → auto-switch the conflict chain to the new antenna, all RX slices on that chain follow. Toast appears: "Slice B's antenna changed from ANT2 to ANT3. [Undo]".
  ● Conflict chain contains the TX-bound slice → confirmation dialog: "Adding Slice C on ANT3 will switch your TX-bound slice (Slice A) from ANT1 to ANT3. Confirm?" with the four explicit options.
  ● Operator can change the default policy in Setup → Antenna → "Conflict policy: [Auto-switch (RX only safe) ✓] [Always ask] [Auto-switch (any)]".

§4 Toast notification — silent antenna re-route

✓
Antenna auto-switched
Slice B moved from ANT2 to ANT3 to fit Slice C on Chain 1.

Non-blocking, lower-right, auto-dismisses after 8 seconds. Undo button reverts the antenna and removes the newly-added slice (or rejects its antenna preference and falls back to the chain's existing antenna).
