ADC / DDC auto-routing and the WIDE indicator
Proposed rule. Filter ranges and behaviour sourced from deskhpsdr alex.h + new_protocol.c and Thetis console.cs. Colours are real StyleConstants / VfoWidget::sliceColor values.
1. The unit of conflict is a filter range, not a band
This is the correction that makes the whole thing work. The ANAN band-pass bank covers frequency ranges spanning several bands, so two slices only conflict when they fall in different ranges. Comparing band names (what we do today) needlessly bypasses pairs the hardware covers with one filter.

So 20m + 15m share one filter (both inside 11.0-22.0 MHz) and need no compromise at all. 40m + 30m likewise. But 20m + 40m land in different ranges and genuinely cannot share one chain.

Three ranges, two chains. Only then does a chain bypass, and only the chain that had to absorb two ranges. Chain 1 keeps its filter. WIDE is the last resort, not the first answer.
3. WIDE appears only on the pans fed by a bypassed chain
Case C above. Slices A and C sit on the bypassed chain 0, so their pans carry WIDE. Slice B is on the filtered chain 1 and stays clean. The badge is per-pan because bypass is per-chain, not per-radio. Clicking WIDE opens the existing Filter Policy dialog.

Hover text on the badge: "BYPASS: chain 0 carries 20/15 + 6m. Move a slice to ANT2 or close one to restore filtering." It says what to do, not just what happened.
4. Setup shows the routing, and lets you pin it
Thetis exposes this as per-DDC radio buttons the operator sets by hand. We keep that (the page already exists) and add an Auto row, so the operator can pin what they care about and let the router place the rest. Pins always win.
