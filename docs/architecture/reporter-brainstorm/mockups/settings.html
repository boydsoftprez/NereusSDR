WHAT THIS IS

Roundtable's settings as a Spot Hub tab, beside FreeDV and PSK Reporter, in the same layout: a server group with Auto-Start and Start, then the same "Hide my station" wording FreeDV uses.

The first two groups are set on the Core, so the phone sees the same. Preferences are this computer only.

Hide my station is the one hide switch. Hidden means nothing about you leaves the Core. You can still read every room. The preview shows how others see you.

The mute list lives on the Core so a station muted here is muted on the phone too.

New, for your veto: Delete my archive, the away choice (keep reporting is the default, as decided), and highlight messages that name me.
