WHAT THIS IS

Your private record of every time you were on the air. Opened from the Roundtable panel header (Archive), the phone's Roundtable page, or the public web page after you prove your station key.

A session is one stretch on the air on one frequency. Each one lists every station that reported you.

WATCHED is how long a station kept reporting you, which is not how long you talked. The timeline shows the difference: amber hatching is when you were keyed and that station had lost you (their side, propagation). Plain gaps are when you were not keyed.

Each station's own raw readings of you sit on its own row. Nothing is averaged across stations. Click a row to see that station's readings over the session.

Empty sessions are kept. Nobody heard you, and that is recorded plainly.

Signal history is kept for good. Message text is removed after one year; the session and readings stay.

AWAY rows are stations whose Core was reporting with nobody at the radio.
