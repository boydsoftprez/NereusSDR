NereusSDR on iPhone and iPad

Roundtable

Roundtable drawn on the app itself: the same band, flags, toolbar, panels, gauges, dial and Setup pages as the iPhone board, with Roundtable added. Your calls applied: a list or link reads a frequency and only a tag on the band (or Tune A here) tunes; PTT is press and hold while the sheet is open, 24 pt clear of the message box; while you transmit, the readings of you take the room and today's keyed gauges stay where they are; with a tuning dial on, the sheet stops above a strip with PTT on the left and the dial on the right; swipes on the Panadapter tab that you map in Setup, with the iPad's edges Off and its swipe up widening the Roundtable applet; the first time a station opens Roundtable, one sheet says what goes public, with Hide my station on it; every reading shows its age and dims past 20 seconds, a room dims once nothing in it is newer than a minute, and every Roundtable screen carries one line saying whether Roundtable is live; only a message that names you lights the flag line, as @1, and it clears once that message has been on screen; on a crowded band, tags that would overlap fold into one +N tag that lists them; the sheet and the iPad applet are one scroll with the message box pinned, and messages that arrive while you read further up show as an "N new" pill rather than moving the page; no text under 11 pt, and every line holds 4.5:1 against its ground.

Signals, callsigns, grids, distances and readings are examples. Each screen is its own phone and works: tap the flag's Roundtable line, the tags, the handle, Busy elsewhere, ⋯ on a message, and hold PTT with the sheet open. The look is the app's workspace one (the iPhone and iPad restyle, merged 2026-10-23), with Roundtable's talking, unknown and doubled colors placed on its green, amber and red.

Roundtable on the band

Tags sit along the foot of the spectrum, clear of the flags, one per frequency with people on it. Shape and color both say what is happening: a filled dot talking, a ringed dot an unknown talker, a striped diamond two stations doubled, a hollow ring quiet. Each slice's flag gets one Roundtable line for the frequency it is on.

Tags and the flag line

A tap on a tag tunes the active slice there, as a tap on the band does. The flag line reads "Roundtable · 9 · K4ABC", the dot saying K4ABC is talking, with @1 when a message names you; a tap opens the sheet.

The sheet, half height

The talker, then every station's own reading, nearest first, with distance and tuning offset; you in cyan. PTT shrinks to the left of the message box and becomes press and hold.

Full height

A tap on the handle. Earlier turns and the messages follow the readings in one scroll; the box stays pinned. A message that names you is marked.

Transmitting with Roundtable

Today's keyed gauges on the waterfall (forward power, SWR, mic level) are untouched. While you transmit, the sheet sits below them and holds one TX line and every station's reading of you; earlier turns wait. With the knob or the thumbwheel on, the sheet stops above a strip that keeps PTT on the left and the dial on the right, where they always are.

Keyed, sheet closed

The gauges as today. The flag line turns to "9 hear you"; a tap opens the sheet below the gauges.

Keyed, sheet open

Held PTT. The sheet drops below the gauges; the TX line and readings of you fill it. Let go and it returns to where it was.

Knob on, sheet open

The sheet stops above a 92 pt strip: PTT left at full size, the knob right, drawn smaller. The knob still tunes slice A while you read.

Thumbwheel on, keyed

Keyed with a dial on, the message box steps aside and your draft is kept for when you let go.

The first time: Roundtable is public

The first time a station opens Roundtable, from the flag line, a swipe or Tools, one sheet says plainly what goes public, with Hide my station right there. Asked once per station: the Core keeps the answer, so a phone, an iPad and the desktop on the same station ask once between them.

What goes public

Callsign and grid, your readings of each talker, everyone's readings of you, and your messages. Your archive stays private. Hiding stops what your station sends; others can still hear you and may name you when you talk. Go live, or stay hidden.

Hide my station, on the sheet

The switch on: Stay hidden becomes the main button. Either button works whatever the switch says.

Hidden

You see every room and reading. Your own reading shows to you only, marked NOT SENT, and you can't post. Go live is one tap.

Answered on another device

The station went live from the desktop, so the phone doesn't ask; it says so once.

Freshness and connection

A reading 40 seconds old should not look live. Each reading shows its age in its own column; past 20 seconds the row dims, its bar stops moving and the age turns amber. In the list, a room dims once nothing in it is newer than a minute. One line under the room's header says whether Roundtable is live: it is Roundtable's own link from the Core, so the radio link pill in the toolbar is unchanged.

Live, one reading going old

G0ABC faded out 34 seconds ago: dimmed, age in amber. The rest show a few seconds and keep refreshing.

Reconnecting

The line counts how long nothing new has come; readings age, then dim at 20 seconds; Send waits and your draft stays. Loops: drops, catches up, drops.

Offline

The flag line says offline; tags on the band grey out as last known; every reading dims with its age. Send is off and your draft stays in the box.

Connecting, at first start

No tags yet; the flag line says connecting; the sheet holds placeholder rows until the Core reaches Roundtable. Loops: connecting, then live.

Mentions

A busy frequency posts all the time, so a light for every new message would never go out. Only a message that names your callsign counts: the flag line shows @1, and the Tools list shows it beside the room. Other new messages show only inside the room ("4, 2 new"). Opening the room does not clear the count; the message that names you has to be on screen for a moment, so it can't slip past under newer ones.

Chatter, flag line quiet

Messages keep coming on 7.236.400, but none name you since you last looked. The flag line stays as it is: who is talking and how many are here.

Someone names you

K4ABC posts "N0CALL you are next." The flag line adds @1; a second mention makes it @2. Tap the line to open the room.

Opened, mention below

The room opens on the readings as always. The message that names you is further down, so a pill above the box says so; the flag line still shows @1. Tap the pill.

Read

The pill scrolled to it. Once it has been on screen for a moment, "names you" and @1 go away; the message keeps its cyan mark. Read on one device, it is read on all of them.

New messages while you read

Each Roundtable panel is one scroll, with the message box pinned below it. Nothing moves while you read further up: messages that come in below the view wait, and a pill above the box counts them. At the newest message, the view follows each new one as it comes. These rooms keep talking: another message arrives every few seconds.

Reading, two arrive

You are reading K4ABC's readings when two messages come in. The readings stay where they are, and the pill says "2 new". It counts up as more arrive.

Tap "2 new"

The pill scrolls to the newest. Each new message has a blue edge and "new" until it has been on screen for a moment.

At the newest, it follows

Already at the bottom of the messages, a new one scrolls into view on its own, and no pill appears. A message that names you still gets the @ pill first.

Typing while one arrives

Your draft stays in the box and the view stays where you left it. "1 new" waits above the box until you look.

A crowded band

On a busy evening 40 m can carry dozens of rooms, and their tags would pile on top of each other. Tags that would overlap fold into one tag: its glyphs show what is inside, and +N says how many. A fold holds two or more; a single neighbor just moves aside. If the folded tags still do not fit across the band, the folds widen until they do. Your slice's room never folds, and neither does a room where a message names you. Zoom in and the band spreads out, so the folds open by themselves; there is no separate zoom setting.

Fourteen rooms in 45 kHz

Neighbors that would overlap become +2 and +4. K4ABC stays out because slice A is there; W4ABC stays out because a message there names you.

A tap on +N

A short list of the rooms inside, by frequency, with who is talking and how many are there. A row tunes slice A, as a tag does; a tap anywhere else closes it.

Zoomed in

Two taps on + and the band spreads out: most folds open into their own tags. Rooms only 2.5 kHz apart can still share one until you zoom further.

Tuned from the list

K8ABC picked from a fold: slice A moves to 7.253, the flag line follows, and that room's tag now stands on its own while its old neighbors stay folded.

Swipes on the panadapter

Extra ways in, never the only way: every page still has its button. Panadapter tab only, and nothing happens at the edges while a sheet or panel is up. You map each swipe in Setup, General, Navigation, beside the tuning dial; choices are Off, RX panel, TX panel, Roundtable on my frequency, Roundtable on all frequencies, Spot Hub and Tools. Remapping changes the swipe only.

Where a swipe starts

Drawn here only: 18 pt at each edge of the band, and the tab bar. A drag that starts anywhere else moves the band, as today.

From the left edge: RX panel

The same panel the toolbar's left button opens, from the same side. Swipe it back or tap the band to close; a short drag springs back.

From the right edge: TX panel

The toolbar's right button's panel. Opening it this way never keys the radio.

Up from the tab bar: Roundtable

The sheet for your frequency, at half height, as the flag line opens it.

Setup, General, Navigation

Under the tuning dial: one row per swipe, saved on this phone. Reset to defaults lights once you change one; until then it shows, dimmed.

Picking what a swipe does

The seven choices. Off gives the edge back to dragging the band.

Naming, muting and reporting

Readings show even when the talker isn't on NereusSDR; listeners name each turn, and every name given is shown, never one winner. Mute is for you only; Report goes to the review list, and nothing is hidden by report counts.

Who is talking?

Slice B on 7.249: an unknown talker. Pick from names used on this frequency or type one.

Naming a turn after it ends

An unknown earlier turn keeps its Name button, so you can name it once you work out who it was.

⋯ on a message

Mute folds that station's messages away for you only; nobody is told. Report, or copy the text.

Report

A reason if you want to give one, including a wrong name on a turn. The station isn't told who reported.
