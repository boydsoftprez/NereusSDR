PUBLIC PAGE

Built for a couple hundred rooms

Cards are gone. Each room is one 30 px row, grouped by band, with the open room beside the list. A laptop screen shows about 20 rooms at once where the cards showed 6, and nothing is lost: talker, how long, how many hear them, their readings and the last message are all on the row.

The band buttons carry room counts and a small bar of how many are talking (green) or unknown (amber). Pick a band and a band map appears: every room at its spot on the band, the stem as tall as the number of stations hearing it. Click one to open it.

Quiet rooms fold away under each band with a count, so a band with 50 rooms shows the 20 that are live.

Search finds a callsign wherever it appears: talking, reading or posting. Type a frequency to jump to it.

The little strip on each row is one bar per station's own reading, nearest first. Never averaged, never ranked.

Keyboard: / goes to search, the arrow keys move through the rooms and each one shows as you land on it, Enter goes into the room (its earlier turns are a list the arrows walk), and Esc steps back: room to list, then clears the search, then the band.

State has a shape as well as a color: filled dot talking, ringed dot unknown, striped diamond doubled, hollow ring quiet.

The look is the program's own 2026.10.1 workspace: its title bar, inset fields, borders and glass selection, as the website's simulated window now draws them.
