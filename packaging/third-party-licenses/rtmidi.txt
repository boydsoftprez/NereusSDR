RtMidi 6.0.0, https://github.com/thestk/rtmidi
Used by the NereusSDR desktop app for MIDI controller input. Not in the Core.

Changed from upstream: third_party/rtmidi/RtMidi.cpp only. throw() is removed from
getCoreMidiClientSingleton in MidiInCore and MidiOutCore, so that an RtMidiError
propagates instead of ending the program, and one error message names MidiOutCore.
The change is AetherSDR commit 5769f480. docs/attribution/RTMIDI-PROVENANCE.md shows
the exact lines.
