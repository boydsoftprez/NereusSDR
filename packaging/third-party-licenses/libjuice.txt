NereusSDR uses libjuice through libdatachannel as the R3 direct ICE backend.
The pinned source is fetched from:

  https://github.com/paullouisageneau/libjuice/tree/3c40a3545b6b1b62c7adee7f8f2bd58aa290afd6

libjuice is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The complete
license is supplied in MPLv2.txt. This backend does not supply the R5 TURN
TCP/TLS fallback; that deployment acceptance remains separate.

Modifications (MPL-2.0 section 3.1): the desktop/Core build applies three
numbered patches to build-owned copies of the pinned source in
cmake/NereusRemoteMedia.cmake:

  cmake/patches/libjuice-0001-give-turn-allocations-back.patch
  cmake/patches/libjuice-0002-bounded-turn-release-lifecycle.patch
  cmake/patches/libjuice-0003-keep-poll-worker-with-retained-agents.patch

The first changes src/agent.c to give TURN allocations back with an
authenticated Refresh of LIFETIME 0 when an agent is destroyed. The second
changes include/juice/juice.h, src/agent.c, src/agent.h and src/juice.c. It
provides a nonblocking begin/status close API, retains the original socket
and credentials for bounded authenticated release retries, and keeps the
agent alive until release and resolver completion. Normal ICE traffic and
callbacks stop during closing. The old direct-destroy best-effort behavior
remains for legacy callers; a resolver test hook is excluded from release
builds. The third changes src/conn_poll.c to retain the poll worker while
finished agents still own the registry, allowing a new agent to wake it. It
adds no worker or timeout change.

These changes are licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, like
the covered files they modify, which retain their upstream notices. The
exact patches are supplied with NereusSDR's source and its written source
offer (SOURCE-OFFER.txt).

The NereusSDR for iPhone and iPad app applies its three numbered patches to
the same pinned revision under ios/NereusKit/Sources/CJuice/ through
ios/scripts/vendor-sources.sh libjuice:

  ios/patches/libjuice/0001-give-turn-allocations-back.patch
  ios/patches/libjuice/0002-bounded-turn-release-lifecycle.patch
  ios/patches/libjuice/0003-keep-poll-worker-with-retained-agents.patch

The first two provide allocation release and the bounded closing lifecycle
described above. The third changes src/conn_poll.c to retain the poll worker
while finished agents still own the registry, allowing a new agent to wake
it. It adds no worker or timeout change. Together they modify
include/juice/juice.h, src/agent.c, src/agent.h, src/juice.c and
src/conn_poll.c. The patches and modified covered files remain licensed under
the Mozilla Public License 2.0 and are published with NereusSDR's source.
