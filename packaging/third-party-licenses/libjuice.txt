NereusSDR uses libjuice through libdatachannel as the R3 direct ICE backend.
The pinned source is fetched from:

  https://github.com/paullouisageneau/libjuice/tree/3c40a3545b6b1b62c7adee7f8f2bd58aa290afd6

libjuice is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The complete
license is supplied in MPLv2.txt. This backend does not supply the R5 TURN
TCP/TLS fallback; that deployment acceptance remains separate.

Modifications (MPL-2.0 section 3.1): NereusSDR applies two numbered patches
to build-owned copies of the pinned source in cmake/NereusRemoteMedia.cmake:

  cmake/patches/libjuice-0001-give-turn-allocations-back.patch
  cmake/patches/libjuice-0002-bounded-turn-release-lifecycle.patch

The first changes src/agent.c to give TURN allocations back with an
authenticated Refresh of LIFETIME 0 when an agent is destroyed. The second
changes include/juice/juice.h, src/agent.c, src/agent.h and src/juice.c. It
provides a nonblocking begin/status close API, retains the original socket
and credentials for bounded authenticated release retries, and keeps the
agent alive until release and resolver completion. Normal ICE traffic and
callbacks stop during closing. The old direct-destroy best-effort behavior
remains for legacy callers; a resolver test hook is excluded from release
builds.
