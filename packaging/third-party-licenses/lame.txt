NereusSDR links LAME as a separate shared library and does not change its code:
libmp3lame.so.0 from the system package on Linux (libmp3lame0), the
Homebrew lame dylib on macOS (on Intel Macs, LAME 3.100 built from its
source tarball and bundled in the app; its code is unchanged, and as in
Homebrew's build the undefined name lame_init_old is left out of
include/libmp3lame.sym, the library's export list), and libmp3lame-0.dll
on Windows, LAME 3.100 built from the same source tarball with the MSYS2
MinGW toolchain and shipped beside the program; its code is unchanged, it
is configured without LAME's decoder, and the names that leaves undefined
(lame_init_old and the hip_* and lame_decode* decoder functions) are left
out of include/libmp3lame.sym. A
user may replace that library with a modified build of LAME with the
same interface. NereusSDR uses LAME's encoder only; MP3 files are decoded
with minimp3 (`minimp3.txt`).
